01 About

Six years in sales.
Ten years on stage.

I'm Marius Bracke. For over six years I've been at home in B2B sales — across software, IT, real estate, and culture. Most recently as Senior Sales Executive at UpVisit, before that at talque, twotickets, and Guilty76.

In parallel, for over ten years I've been building event formats: Music Sneak, Sofar Sounds Frankfurt & Berlin, Cosmo Social Club. Sounds like two worlds. It's not. A concert in front of 200 people and a pitch to three investors run on the same rules: trust, timing, perception. What draws people in or pushes them away rarely has to do with the product. It has to do with whether something fits — and whether someone knows the mechanics behind it.

With the founders I work with today, I see the same pattern again and again: lots of activity, little progress. Decisions go in circles. No one can say where it's really stuck. I'm not the next consultant with a deck. I come in, find the bottleneck, and clear it — like an internal mind, just without the operational blindness.

02 How I work

Four principles
I hold myself to.

01

Start with the end.

We begin with the goal — not the method. What should be different in 12 months? Only then do we talk about the path there.

02

Strategy without execution is theory.

I don't deliver concepts to file away. I actively support execution, help build the systems, and think in outcomes — not recommendations.

03

Work the bottleneck — not the framework.

I don't follow a fixed framework. I find what's really stuck — and clear exactly that. Everything else is effort without impact.

04

Done is better than perfect.

A process that lives is worth more than a perfect one that never starts. We build things that work — and sharpen them along the way.
